# THE ABANDONED MALL — Floor 4 Complete Design
## WHAT / WHY / HOW
- **WHAT:** Fully designed dungeon floor (Floor 4 of 10)
- **WHY:** First psychological threat floor, introduces Aberration faction
- **HOW:** Modern capitalism made mythic, consumption literal, glitch mechanics

---

## FLOOR ARCHITECTURE

**Shape:** Diamond-shaped layout on hex grid (difficult geometry)  
**Theme:** Abandoned shopping center — modern capitalism made mythic  
**Levels:** 
- Main floor (stores, escalators)
- Sub level (parking garage)
- Secondary level (food court)

**Key Features:**
- **Central Escalator:** Broken mechanism requiring parts to fix. Moves unpredictably — wrong step carries player to different encounter.
- **Four Pillars:** Each shows different version of mall on screen. Only one real (leads to parts). Others are traps — dead ends, ambushes, infinite loops.
- **Advertisement Traps:** Storefronts that flicker between products and lethal illusions. Stay too long, screen shows your reflection, triggers Mirror Self encounter.

---

## ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS

### The Consumption Loop
- Stores restock with player desires
- Each item examined drains small HP
- Purchase requires Quiddity (literally buying with life)

### The Food Court
- Abandoned restaurants serve memories
- Eating heals but causes "Nostalgia" status (vulnerable to emotion attacks)

### The Parking Garage
- Broken cars, leaking fluids
- "Oil Slick" terrain — movement random
- Car alarms attract encounters

## FLOOR 4 ENEMIES (10 Aberration Enemies)

### Floors 1-2 (Starter Tier) — Already in faction roster

### Floors 3-4 (Early Tier) — 6 Enemies
**HP Range: 6-12**

#### 4. The Duplicate
**HP:** 8 | **Pattern:** Special→Melee→Defend→Special | **Weight:** Uncommon  
**Mechanic: Freeze Ceiling** — Special = Player's maximum Attention is locked at current value for 2 turns. Can spend Attention freely below the cap, but cannot rise above it. Forces resource management with fixed ceiling.  
**Visual:** Hollow player-shaped void with Attention meter frozen mid-glow, frost creeping across the numbers.

#### 5. The Loop
**HP:** 7 | **Pattern:** Defend→Special→Melee→Defend | **Weight:** Rare  
**Mechanic: Cycle** — Special = Forces card type rotation. Player must play: Attack → Summon/Trap → Special/Field → repeat. Breaking cycle deals 3 damage.  
**Visual:** Three-faced entity, each face glowing color of required card type, rotating endlessly.

#### 6. The Forgotten
**HP:** 6 | **Pattern:** Special→Flee→Special | **Weight:** Uncommon  
**Mechanic: Oblivion** — Special = Player "forgets" highest-cost card in hand (cannot play it this combat). Card returns only after Forgotten dies or player takes damage from another source.  
**Visual:** Shadow clinging to player's shadow, faceless mouth where memory should be.

### Floors 5-6 (Mid Tier) — 6 Enemies
**HP Range: 8-15**

#### 7. The Lag
**HP:** 10 | **Pattern:** Special→Defend→Melee→Special | **Weight:** Common  
**Mechanic: Delay** — Special = Player's next card resolves **one turn late**. If player plays another card before queue resolves, both fizzle. Forces planning or chaos.  
**Visual:** Transparent duplicate of player, always one turn behind.

#### 8. The Autosave / Rollback
**HP:** 12 | **Pattern:** Defend→Special→Defend→Special | **Weight:** Rare  
**Mechanic: Rollback** — Special = Records player's state (HP, Attention, hand). At start of next Enemy phase, **forces player back to saved state**. Progress undone. Healing reverted. Gains lost.  
**Visual:** Flickering static silhouette from one turn ago, pulling player back.

#### 9. The Cursor
**HP:** 9 | **Pattern:** Melee→Special→Melee→Special | **Weight:** Uncommon  
**Mechanic: Select** — Special = Forces player to discard 2 cards, then **auto-plays** highest-cost remaining card (player chooses target only).  
**Visual:** Disembodied pointing hand, glowing selection box on player's cards.

#### 10. The Bug
**HP:** 8 | **Pattern:** Special→Special→Melee→Defend | **Weight:** Rare  
**Mechanic: Stack** — Special = Adds "Error" card to hand (0 cost, 0 effect, cannot be discarded except by playing). Playing Error deals 1 damage but removes it. Errors clog hand.  
**Visual:** Corrupted pixelated mass, fragments of code floating around it.

## MIRROR SELF (Floor 4 Boss)

**Classification:** Aberration (not Construct)  
**HP:** Equal to player's current HP (perfect mirror)  
**Pattern:** Attack → Defend → Special

**Abilities:**
| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Attack: Attention Value** | Damage scales with player engagement/fear. More you look, harder it hits. |
| **Defend: Reflection Trap** | Creates trap triggering on Direct Spell cast. Forces indirect tactics. |
| **Special: Clone** | Copies player's last played card, throws it back at them. |

**Horror Mechanic:** Not killing you — *replacing* you. The default state, unchosen pattern, escaped loop returning.  
**Visual:** Almost you. Reflection slightly delayed. Smile too wide. Eyes don't track right.
