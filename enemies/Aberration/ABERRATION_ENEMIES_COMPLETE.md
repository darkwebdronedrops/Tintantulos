| Type | Visual Theme | What They Do | Feel |
|------|--------------|--------------|------|

## BOSS ENEMIES — 5 Total

### BOSS 1: The Consumption (Glitch/Error) — Floor 3 Mini-Boss
**HP:** 30 | **Pattern:** Melee→Special→Defend→Melee

**Glitch Mechanic — Devour:**
- Special = Steals 1 gear piece or adds "Half-Digested Junk" to hand (0-cost, does nothing)
- Heals 5 HP every Special regardless
- **Exploitable:** Fast kill before hand pollution becomes unmanageable. Don't hoard gear.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Indigestion** — If player has 3+ Junk cards, Consumption chokes (skips next turn)

**Visual:** Gaping maw surrounded by grasping tendrils. The hunger that eats your inventory.

---

### BOSS 2: The Confluence (Glitch/Mirror) — Floor 6
**HP:** 45 | **Pattern:** Open→Command→Cascade→Defend

**Glitch Mechanic — Faction Echo:**
- Open = Summons 1 random faction enemy at 40% HP (echo — no rewards, vanishes on boss death)
- Command = All echoes act immediately
- Cascade = Damage scales with number of living echoes
- **Exploitable:** Kill echoes fast, or ignore them and burst boss (echoes vanish when boss dies)

**Hard Counter:**
- **Dissolution** — If player plays Aberration card, all echoes become confused (50% chance to attack boss)

**Visual:** Tear in space where five rivers meet — oil, blood, mercury, ash, mirror-silver. The factions converge.

---

### BOSS 3: The Replacement (Glitch/Void) — Floor 9
**HP:** 80 | **Integrity:** 20 (Ticks to game over) | **Pattern:** Approach→Integrate→Replace

**Glitch Mechanic — Colonization:**
- Integrate deals "Ticks" (not HP damage). Thresholds:
  - 5 Ticks: One card "Assimilated" (gray border, optimistic flavor text)
  - 10 Ticks: Portrait flickers between player and boss
  - 15 Ticks: Can only play cards boss has seen before
  - 20 Ticks: **Full Replacement** — Game Over
- Replace heals HP equal to current Ticks
- **Exploitable:** Burst damage before Ticks accumulate, or survive to 19 Ticks and kill

**Hard Counter:**
- **Novelty** — Playing a card type the boss has NEVER seen removes 3 Ticks

**Visual:** Humanoid-shaped absence. Where it stands, you see yourself. The longer you look, the more right it looks.

**Theme:** *Not killed. Replaced.*

### BOSS 5: The Cano Protocol (Glitch/All) — Secret Boss
**HP:** 250 | **Pattern:** Remember→Reflect→Transcend

**Glitch Mechanic — Meta-Narrative:**
- Knows: run count, bosses killed, cards favored, play patterns
- Remember = Uses card from player's most-played deck
- Reflect = Copies last played card with "Echo" prefix
- Transcend = Changes one game rule for 1 turn ("Cards cost Health" / "Draw from discard" / "Enemies are allies")
- Phase 2: Shows all previous runs (win/loss, playtime)
- Phase 3: Offers "Surrender" (peaceful death, unlocks cutscene) or "Persist" (boss +50 HP)
- **Kill:** Deal 250 in one turn, or accept Surrender then betray (250 damage)

**Hard Counter:**
- **Amnesia** — Playing a card you've NEVER played in any run confuses boss (skips next turn)

**Visual:** Static. Flickering between versions. Speaks in second person.

**Theme:** *The game knows you're playing. The only way out is through — or surrender.*

## GLITCH MECHANICS — DETAILED

### Lag (Time Distortion)
- Delayed card resolution
- Actions that resolve one turn late
- Movement deferred to next turn
- Player can walk off edges before position updates

### Mirror (Identity Theft)
- Copies player cards, stats, appearance
- Steals most-used cards
- Forces player to play what enemy has seen
- Portrait flickers between identities

### Error (Information Corruption)
- Adds junk/blank/error cards to hand
- Fake enemy intents
- Wrong UI information (hidden HP, costs, intents)
- Randomized grades/statuses

### Void (Memory/Choice Erasure)
- Forgets card types
- Removes options
- Replays previous combats
- Highlights wrong choices as "best"
- Pressure stacks that carry between floors

## DESIGN NOTES

### What Makes Aberrations Different
1. **System-level attacks** — They don't damage HP directly; they damage the interface, the information, the choices
2. **Post-combat consequences** — Some effects (Pressure stacks, fake intents, deck pollution) carry between floors
3. **The horror of uncertainty** — "Is this real? Is the UI lying? Did I actually choose that?"
4. **Novelty as counterplay** — The only consistent defense is doing something the Aberration hasn't seen
5. **Meta-awareness** — High-tier aberrations (Cano Protocol, The Certainty) know they're in a game

### Player Experience
- **Early:** Confusion, mild annoyance, learning to read true information
- **Mid:** Paranoia, second-guessing every UI element, adapting to delayed effects
- **Late:** Existential dread — the game itself is unreliable, memory is suspect, identity blurs
- **Bosses:** The mirror, the loop, the certainty, the protocol — each a different flavor of "you are not in control"
