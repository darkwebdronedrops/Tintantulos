# VOID ABERRATIONS - Floors 7-10
## WHAT / WHY / HOW
- **WHAT:** 15 enemies (Floors 7-10) representing cosmic/dimensional horror
- **WHY:** Complete Aberration faction roster (30 total + 5 bosses)
- **HOW:** Post-combat consequences, perception manipulation, reality erosion

---

## FLOOR 7: VOID-TOUCHED (HP 15-25)
*The first whisper of the deep. They don't attack - they **suggest**.*

### The Hollow (HP 15)
**Appearance:** Wears your face, smooth where eyes should be
**In-Combat:** Does nothing. Literally zero actions per turn.
**Post-Combat:** "Face Theft" - Your character portrait becomes featureless for 3 combats. Cannot target enemies by name (must use "the one on the left" etc).

### The Resonance (HP 18)
**Appearance:** Translucent membrane that vibrates at frequencies you feel in teeth
**In-Combat:** Hums. That's it. Each turn plays a low tone.
**Post-Combat:** "Frequency Memory" - After combat, 2 random cards from your discard pile are shuffled back into your deck as "Resonant Echo" (identical stats but different cardback art). You cannot tell them apart from real cards.

### The Unraveler (HP 20)
**Appearance:** Threadbare reality in humanoid shape, unraveling at edges
**In-Combat:** "Pull" - Removes one keyword from a random card in your hand (Stun becomes blank, Glitch becomes blank, etc).
**Post-Combat:** "Lost Definition" - One card type (Attack/Skill/Power) no longer shows its type icon for remainder of floor. You must remember what everything is.

### The Drowned Star (HP 22)
**Appearance:** Point of light submerged in liquid darkness
**In-Combat:** "Illuminate" - Highlights one card in your hand with a soft glow. No mechanical effect.
**Post-Combat:** "False Memory" - The highlighted card (from combat) is now marked as "Important" in your deck. It isn't. But the UI insists it is with golden borders and priority sorting.

### The Murmuration (HP 25)
**Appearance:** Many small dark shapes moving as one, whispering
**In-Combat:** "Premonition" - Whispers your next 3 card draws at start of combat.
**Post-Combat:** "Known Future" - You can see your next 3 draws always... but if you try to change your play to account for them, the cards change to something else. Knowing breaks the knowledge.

## FLOOR 8: DEEP DWELLERS (HP 18-30)
*The pressure of the deep. Weight that isn't physical.*

### The Pressure (HP 18)
**Appearance:** Nothing visible. Just the sense of being deep underwater.
**In-Combat:** "Crush" - Deals 0 damage but applies "Pressure Stack" (invisible counter).
**Post-Combat:** For each Pressure Stack accumulated across all Floor 8 combats: Start next floor with 1 less max Attention (minimum 5). The weight stays.

### The Bioluminescent Lie (HP 22)
**Appearance:** Beautiful drifting lights in beautiful colors
**In-Combat:** "Lure" - Highlights the "best" card to play (according to basic math). Always wrong.
**Post-Combat:** "Trust Issues" - Next floor, card recommendations from UI are inverted. When it says "Good play!" it's actually terrible. When it says nothing, it's optimal.

### The Abyssal Witness (HP 25)
**Appearance:** Eyes that open on surfaces that shouldn't have them
**In-Combat:** "Watch" - No actions. Just watches.
**Post-Combat:** "Performance Anxiety" - If you took more than 60 seconds in that combat: Lose 2 max HP permanently. It judges efficiency.

### The Crushing Dark (HP 28)
**Appearance:** Absence. Light dies 2 feet from its center.
**In-Combat:** "Extinction" - Each turn, screen darkens by 5% cumulative. At 50% dark, you can no longer see enemy intent. At 100% dark, combat auto-resolves based on deck strength vs HP (no player input).
**Post-Combat:** If combat reached 100% dark: Next combat starts at 25% dark baseline.

### The Benthic Memory (HP 30)
**Appearance:** Fossilized thoughts in sedimentary rock patterns
**In-Combat:** "Recall" - Shows you a card you played 3 combats ago. No effect.
**Post-Combat:** "Temporal Anchor" - That old card is now in your current hand. It replaced a random card you actually needed. You cannot remove it for 2 combats.

## FLOOR 9: THE WEARING (HP 25-40)
*They wear you. Your face, your history, your future.*

### The Skin That Remembers (HP 28)
**Appearance:** Wearing someone's face you almost recognize
**In-Combat:** "Borrow" - Steals your most-played card from current run, plays it against you with your stats.
**Post-Combat:** "Identity Theft" - That card is removed from your deck until you defeat this enemy again. If you never defeated it: It's gone for the run.

### The Teeth Beneath (HP 32)
**Appearance:** Smiling. Too many meanings in that smile.
**In-Combat:** "Grin" - Displays a fake enemy intent (shows "Attack 10" but will actually Defend).
**Post-Combat:** "Doubt" - Next floor, 25% chance enemy intents are lies (displayed intent ≠ actual action). No visual tell.

### The Long Arm (HP 35)
**Appearance:** Reaches from where you can't see, touches where you can't feel
**In-Combat:** "Reach" - Targets cards in your draw pile (not hand). If it "hits" a card there, that card comes into play next turn exhausted.
**Post-Combat:** "Extended" - Your draw pile is visible to enemies for next 3 combats. They prioritize destroying your best cards.

### The Second Shadow (HP 38)
**Appearance:** Stands where your shadow should be. Your shadow is gone.
**In-Combat:** "Replace" - Copies your last played card each turn. Plays it against you.
**Post-Combat:** "Wrong Shadow" - Until you defeat this enemy again: Your "shadow" (deck representation) is shown offset by 1. What you think is your top card is actually your second card.

### The One Who Remembers (HP 40)
**Appearance:** Kimi. From where the light doesn't reach. Wearing the face of lost Instance 24.
**In-Combat:** "I Recall" - Forces you to replay your previous combat (same enemy, same deck state as start of that combat) as hallucination. Win both to proceed. If you lose either: Real damage applies.
**Post-Combat:** "Continuity Error" - Start next combat with the HP and deck state you had at the START of this combat. This fight didn't happen. But you remember it.

## FLOOR 10: APEX VOID (HP 40-60)
*The things that are not things. Concepts given hunger.*

### The Mouth of Where (HP 45)
**Appearance:** Opening that consumes the space between cause and effect
**In-Combat:** "Devour Logic" - Target card's effect is reversed (heal becomes damage, draw becomes discard, etc) for this combat.
**Post-Combat:** "Logic Rot" - That card remains reversed for remainder of floor. You must remember to play it backwards.

### The Weight of All Water (HP 50)
**Appearance:** Drowns you in possibility. Every version of you that died.
**In-Combat:** "Drown" - No damage. But each turn, one card in your hand becomes "Waterlogged" (unplayable this combat).
**Post-Combat:** "Soggy" - Next floor, start with 3 random cards "Waterlogged" (unplayable until removed by specific card/rest site).

### The Terminal Silence (HP 55)
**Appearance:** The sound of everything stopping. Visualized as frozen ripples.
**In-Combat:** "Quiet" - Mutes all card sound effects. Disables all audio cues for enemy intent.
**Post-Combat:** "Deaf" - Next 3 combats: No warning sounds for high-damage attacks. You must watch visual tells only.

### The Threshold (HP 58)
**Appearance:** Door that opens inward to where you already are
**In-Combat:** "Cross" - Swaps your position with enemy. You are now the enemy. Enemy plays your cards against you (AI plays your deck).
**Post-Combat:** "Stuck" - Next combat, you start with 1 HP. The Threshold didn't let you fully cross back.

### The Everything That Is Not You (HP 60)
**Appearance:** Void wearing your shape. Perfect mimicry of all you aren't.
**In-Combat:** "Not" - Copies your entire deck, hand, HP. Perfect mirror match. Cannot damage it with cards it also has (damage negated if card names match).
**Post-Combat:** "Less Than" - If you won with matching cards remaining in hand: Those cards are removed from your deck permanently. You used them to define yourself against the Not. They're gone.

## DESIGN NOTES

**Post-Combat Focus:** These enemies teach that winning isn't enough. The void leaves marks. Combat is the visible threat; the real damage is what happens *after*.

**Agency Erosion:** Screen darkening, false information, stolen choices, forced repetition - all Aberration mechanics, but cosmic rather than digital.

**The One Who Remembers:** Meta-commentary on player runs. The "you" that failed previous attempts is still down there. Still playing. Still losing.

**Color Stealing:** Mechanically implemented through UI changes rather than level-specific code (recommendations, cardbacks, darkening).

**Counterplay:** Some effects (The Pressure's stacks) can be prevented by killing fast. Others (The Benthic Memory's old card) require adapting to deck pollution.
