## SWARM MECHANICS — DETAILED

### Visual Feedback
- Goblins emit green glow when Swarm bonus active
- Numbers appear above heads (+1, +2, etc.)
- Goblins huddle together, move as group
- Isolated Goblins cower, shake, emit whimpering sounds

### Swarm Rules
1. **Adjacent = 1 hex away** (including diagonals in hex grid)
2. **Swarm bonuses stack** up to maximum per enemy type
3. **Morale check:** If 50% of Goblins die, remaining must check: 50% chance to Flee next turn
4. **Leadership:** Bosses and Banner Boys prevent Fleeing within aura

### Flee Mechanic
- Flee = Move away from player at max speed
- If cornered while Fleeing, cowers (50% miss, no attack)
- Regroup after 2 turns or when leader arrives

## MORALE SYSTEM

### Morale States
| State | Condition | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Fearless** | Boss present or Banner active | Cannot Flee, +1 damage |
| **Confident** | 3+ Goblins, no losses | Normal behavior |
| **Shaky** | 50% losses or isolated | 25% chance to Flee |
| **Panicked** | 75% losses or leader dead | 75% chance to Flee, 50% miss |

### Breaking Morale
- Kill leader → All Goblins check Morale
- Isolate Goblin → Auto-Shaky
- Survive 3 turns without killing any → Goblins gain Confident

## DESIGN NOTES

### What Makes Goblins Different
1. **Numbers over durability** — Low HP, high quantity
2. **Positioning matters** — Swarm bonuses reward tight formations
3. **Psychological warfare** — Morale system, shrieks, fear effects
4. **Cunning over strength** — Stealth, theft, traps, backstab
5. **Chaotic energy** — Random flee behavior, unpredictable patterns

### Player Experience
- **Early:** Overwhelmed by numbers, learn to AoE
- **Mid:** Manage morale, prioritize leaders/banners
- **Late:** Navigate complex swarm formations, exploit isolation
