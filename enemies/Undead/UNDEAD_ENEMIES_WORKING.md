## BOSS ENEMIES — 5 Total

### BOSS 1: The Unsent Letter (Shroud) — Floor 3 Mini-Boss
**HP:** 90 | **Pattern:** Special→Melee→Defend→Special

**GRASP Mechanic — Unfinished Business:**
- Special = Steal 3 cards from hand, "seal" them in Letter
- Player can "reclaim" by dealing 20 damage in one turn (burning the letter)
- If not reclaimed in 3 turns, cards are lost permanently this combat
- **Exploitable:** High burst damage reclaims cards. Slow play loses them.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Regret** — If player discards any card, Letter steals 2 more (punishes letting go)

**Visual:** Floating envelope of skin, sealing wax made of dried tears, handwriting that shifts when read

---

### BOSS 2: The Treasury of Bone (Hollow) — Floor 6
**HP:** 140 | **Pattern:** Defend→Special→Melee→Special

**GRASP Mechanic — Compound Interest:**
- Steals 3 Quiddity per turn, stores in Treasury
- Player can "rob" Treasury by dealing damage (1 damage = 1 Quiddity stolen back)
- Treasury has "armor" equal to stored Quiddity (harder to damage when full)
- **Exploitable:** Attack Treasury directly to reclaim Quiddity, or let it fill and burst (risk/reward)

**Hard Counter:**
- **Austerity** — If player plays 3+ cards in one turn, Treasury steals 5 extra Quiddity (punishes greed)

**Visual:** Ribcage vault, coin-eyes that track wealth, marrow flowing like gold

---

### BOSS 3: The Devouring Past (Flesh) — Floor 9
**HP:** 180 | **Pattern:** Melee→Special→Special→Melee

**GRASP Mechanic — Nostalgia:**
- Special = Steal 10 cards from deck, "remember" them (boss gains their effects)
- Player can "forget" by playing cards boss hasn't seen (new experiences erase old)
- If boss "remembers" 30+ cards, it becomes immune to those card types
- **Exploitable:** Play diverse cards (deck variety) to make boss forget. Repetition strengthens it.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Déjà Vu** — If player plays same card twice, boss steals 5 cards (punishes repetition)

**Visual:** Mouth-maw of faces, each a consumed memory, digesting the player's history

---

### BOSS 4: The Recursive Doubt (Shroud/Marrow hybrid) — Secret Boss
**HP:** 120 | **Pattern:** Variable (copies player's last action)

**GRASP Mechanic — Questioning:**
- Copies player's last card type played
- Steals 2 cards of that type from hand/deck
- Player can "answer" by playing card type boss hasn't seen (resets pattern)
- **Exploitable:** Boss is predictable (copies you). Play unexpected cards to break loop.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Uncertainty** — If player hesitates (takes >30 seconds), boss steals 3 cards (punishes doubt)

**Visual:** Mirror-fragment face, questioning gesture, answers that become questions

---

### BOSS 5: The Final Inheritance (All Grafts) — Floor 10 Dragon Alternative
**HP:** 250 | **Pattern:** Rotates Graft each turn (Shroud→Hollow→Flesh→Marrow→repeat)

**GRASP Mechanic — Legacy:**
- Shroud turn: Steal 5 cards from hand
- Hollow turn: Steal 5 Quiddity
- Flesh turn: Steal 10 cards from deck
- Marrow turn: Player "forgets" 1 card type for 1 turn
- **Exploitable:** Player can predict Graft rotation and prepare. Each Graft has counterplay.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Generational Trauma** — If player uses same counter twice, boss adapts (immunity for 2 turns)

**Visual:** Family portrait of death, generations of loss, inheritance of absence

## GRASP MECHANICS — DETAILED

### Visual Feedback
- Shroud: Cards fade from hand, translucent, ghostly
- Hollow: Quiddity drains as visible stream, coins to dust
- Flesh: Cards consumed, torn, digested
- Marrow: Memory fog, card faces blur, names lost

### Recovery Mechanics
| Stolen | Recovery Method | Cost |
|--------|-----------------|------|
| Cards (hand) | Deal damage to Shroud boss | Opportunity cost |
| Quiddity | Kill Hollow enemy | None (drops stolen) |
| Cards (deck) | Reshuffle (automatic) | Time, deck exhaustion |
| Memory | Kill Marrow enemy | None (returns gradually) |

### Permanence
- **Combat-only:** All theft temporary (cards return after combat)
- **Exception:** End of History (deck exhaustion), Generational Trauma (immunity)
- **Feel:** Loss is real in moment, but recoverable with skill

## DESIGN NOTES

### What Makes Undead Different
1. **Personal loss** — Cards stolen from YOUR hand, YOUR deck, YOUR memory
2. **Grief mechanics** — Recovery possible but costly, like mourning
3. **Patient threat** — They don't rush; they take, persist, wait
4. **Resource war** — Combat becomes economic, managing scarcity
5. **Identity erosion** — Marrow enemies attack who you are, not just what you have

### Player Experience
- **Early:** Annoyance at card loss, learning recovery
- **Mid:** Anxiety as deck thins, Quiddity drains
- **Late:** Desperation, then mastery of reclamation
- **Bosses:** Unique mechanics requiring adaptation, not just damage

### Respawn Feel
- Undead don't "spawn" new minions
- They **persist** — kill one, another rises (not new, just... more)
- The same enemies, re-chewed, familiar, inevitable
- "I thought I killed you" — "You did. I remained."
