## BOSS ENEMIES — 5 Total

### BOSS 1: The Interview (Ambition) — Floor 3 Mini-Boss
**HP:** 100 | **Pattern:** Special→Melee→Defend→Special

**PACT Mechanic — The Offer:**
- Special = Offer "partnership" (+2 cards/turn, +3 damage)
- If accepted: Player becomes "employee" — must follow boss's "suggestions" (forced actions) for 3 turns
- **Exploitable:** Decline all offers. Boss weakens each time (desperation). Accept only if desperate.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Performance Review** — If player plays 3+ cards in one turn, boss offers "promotion" (forced acceptance)

**Visual:** Perfect suit, perfect smile, clipboard that writes your name without asking, eyes that see your potential

---

### BOSS 2: The Confession (Certainty) — Floor 6
**HP:** 150 | **Pattern:** Defend→Special→Melee→Special

**PACT Mechanic — The Truth:**
- Special = Reveal "true nature" of one player card (hidden mechanic, synergy, etc.)
- If player uses that knowledge: Card is "understood" (buffed) but cannot be combined with others ("purity of truth")
- **Exploitable:** Use revealed knowledge without playing the card. Let understanding inform other choices.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Dogma** — If player plays same card type 2 turns in a row, boss "confirms" it (forces that type forever)

**Visual:** Confessional booth made of mirrors, voice that knows your secrets, comfort that feels like drowning

---

### BOSS 3: The Embrace (Absolution) — Floor 9
**HP:** 200 | **Pattern:** Melee→Defend→Special→Defend

**PACT Mechanic — The Rest:**
- Special = Offer "mother's embrace" (heal 20 HP, remove all debuffs)
- If accepted: Player cannot take actions for 2 turns ("complete safety")
- **Exploitable:** Accept only at critical HP, then use the 2 turns of safety to plan (mental actions allowed)

**Hard Counter:**
- **Comfort** — If player Defends 2 turns in a row, boss offers "permanent rest" (instant kill if accepted)

**Visual:** Arms that never let go, warmth that cools, the end of all striving, mother's lullaby in a language you forgot

---

### BOSS 4: The Edge (Intensity) — Secret Boss
**HP:** 120 | **Pattern:** Variable (copies player's intensity)

**PACT Mechanic — The Burn:**
- Mirrors player's damage output
- Offers "more" — whatever player just did, ×2 next turn
- If accepted: Player takes 50% of damage dealt (recoil)
- **Exploitable:** Play low, let boss offer, decline, boss weakens from "rejection." Or accept and heal fast.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Addiction** — Each time player accepts, next offer is ×3 but recoil is 75%

**Visual:** Blade that cuts both ways, high that crashes, the moment before the fall, beautiful and sharp

---

### BOSS 5: The Choice (All Offers) — Floor 10 Dragon Alternative
**HP:** 300 | **Pattern:** Rotates through Ambition→Certainty→Absolution→Intensity→repeat

**PACT Mechanic — The Final Offer:**
- Each phase offers that type's ultimate deal
- Player can accept only ONE offer entire fight
- If no offer accepted by phase 4: Boss weakens significantly ("disappointment")
- **Exploitable:** Wait. The cost of waiting is damage, but the final offer is always worst. Or accept early, pay cost, finish fast.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Regret** — Whatever offer accepted, boss uses against player (Ambition = controls player; Certainty = knows all; Absolution = puts to sleep; Intensity = self-damage)

**Visual:** Four faces that are one face, the offer you wanted before you knew wanting, the yes that echoes forever

## PACT MECHANICS — DETAILED

### The Offer Structure
1. **Presentation:** Demon offers something player wants (healing, knowledge, power)
2. **Hidden Cost:** Effect buried in mechanics ("cannot X for Y turns")
3. **Acceptance:** Player uses the benefit
4. **Collection:** Cost activates, often after benefit is consumed

### Visual Language of Temptation
- **Ambition:** Clean lines, improvement, "before and after" glow
- **Certainty:** Clear light, no shadows, "see everything" expansiveness
- **Absolution:** Soft focus, weightlessness, "finally rest" warmth
- **Intensity:** Sharp contrast, saturated colors, "feel everything" electricity

### The 14A Coding
- No explicit sexual content
- All temptation through **metaphor of self-improvement**
- The erotic is in the **dynamic**: being seen, being offered, being wanted
- The horror is in the **realization**: you wanted this, you chose this, you cannot undo

## DESIGN NOTES

### What Makes Demons Different
1. **The offer is real** — They actually give what they promise
2. **The cost is hidden** — Not lied about, just... not emphasized
3. **Player complicity** — You chose. You wanted. You accepted.
4. **Self-destruction as desire** — The player destroys themselves through wanting
5. **The mirror** — Demons show you your own desires, make them achievable, make them costly

### The Erotic Horror (14A)
- **Seduction through understanding** — "I know what you need"
- **Submission as optimization** — "Let me help you be better"
- **The yes that binds** — Consent as trap, desire as prison
- **Pleasure and destruction intertwined** — You feel good as you lose

### Player Experience
- **Early:** Annoyance at "deals," learning to refuse
- **Mid:** Temptation — maybe this offer is worth it?
- **Late:** Desperation — I need this, I'll pay the cost
- **Bosses:** The offer you can't refuse, or the refusal that costs everything
