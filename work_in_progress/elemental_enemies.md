## BOSS ENEMIES — 5 Total

### BOSS 1: The Caldera (Pyre) — Floor 3 Mini-Boss
**HP:** 80 | **Pattern:** Melee→Special→Defend→Special

**CHARGE Mechanic — Thermal Runaway:**
- Gains +2 CHARGE per turn
- At 10 CHARGE: **Meltdown** — 8d8 damage to all, terrain becomes lava for 3 turns
- **Exploitable:** Using Water cards reduces CHARGE by 2. Cooling the boss prevents meltdown.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Flash Freeze** — If player plays 3+ Fire cards in one turn, boss instantly gains 5 CHARGE and attacks twice next turn

**Visual:** Living volcano, cracked obsidian shell, magma veins pulsing with CHARGE level

### BOSS 2: The Abyssal Plane (Abyss) — Floor 6
**HP:** 120 | **Pattern:** Defend→Special→Melee→Special

**CHARGE Mechanic — Pressure Lock:**
- Gains +1 CHARGE per turn, +1 when player moves
- At 15 CHARGE: **Crush** — 10d10 damage, player stunned 1 turn
- **Exploitable:** Standing still for 1 full turn reduces CHARGE by 3. The boss expects movement; stillness confuses it.

**Hard Counter:**
- **Ripple Effect** — If player plays same card type twice in a row, boss duplicates it (free cast against player)

**Visual:** Infinite depth illusion, bioluminescent pressure lines, crushing gravity well

### BOSS 3: The Geometric (Vitreous) — Floor 9
**HP:** 150 | **Pattern:** Special→Melee→Defend→Special→Melee

**CHARGE Mechanic — Perfect Form:**
- Gains +1 CHARGE per turn, +2 when player deals damage

## DESIGN NOTES

### What Makes Elementals Different
1. **Time pressure** — CHARGE builds constantly, creating urgency
2. **Elemental rock-paper-scissors** — Each element counters another
3. **Visual escalation** — Glow, sound, reality distortion at high CHARGE
4. **Exploitable peaks** — Smart players force early release or prevent buildup
5. **Apocalyptic release** — The CHARGE must release, one way or another

### Player Experience
- **Early:** Learn CHARGE patterns, manage basic releases
- **Mid:** Elemental counterplay, forcing suboptimal releases
- **Late:** Complex multi-element fights, managing multiple CHARGEs
- **Bosses:** Unique exploitable mechanics, hard counters requiring deck adaptation
